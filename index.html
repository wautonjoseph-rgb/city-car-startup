Who We Are

ACC is an early-stage automotive startup focused on designing affordable, low-cost vehicles for city commuters. We explore smart, asset-light manufacturing models to make urban mobility accessible, practical, and sustainable.

How We Build

  • Low-Cost Vehicle Architecture: Innovative designs that reduce production and maintenance costs.
  • Contract Manufacturing & Partnerships: Working with experienced manufacturers to bring vehicles to life without massive capital.
  • Urban-Focused Design: Compact, efficient, and built for city streets and daily commuters.

Meet the Founders

ACC was founded by a small, passionate team exploring new ways to make urban vehicles affordable and practical. We’re currently building the team and refining our concept — focused, realistic, and long-term oriented.
